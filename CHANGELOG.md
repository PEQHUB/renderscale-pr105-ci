# 1.4.0-alpha.6

## New versions

- 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge)

## Fixes

- Fixed 1.21.1 NeoForge crashing on launch. [#94](https://github.com/Zolo101/RenderScale/issues/94) 

## Changes

Experimental support for Dynamic Scaling! Set your target FPS, and it'll change the scale factor while you play.

- F3 now shows your current scale factor.
- (1.21.11+) Improve FSR 1.0 performance on scales over 100%.

## Known Issues

- In 1.21.1 you cannot use Dynamic Scaling with shaders due to issues with stuttering.
